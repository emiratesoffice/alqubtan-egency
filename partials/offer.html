Worker benefits

What we offer you

Everything you need for a safe start in the UAE, handled by our office.

  • A real job with a real family

    We match you with a household in the UAE and arrange the contract, the work permit and the residency visa properly.

  • Visa and papers at no cost to you

    The employer pays for the visa, the medical test and the ticket. Nothing is deducted from your salary, ever.

  • Salary into your own account

    Your pay goes to a bank account in your name, so there is a record of every payment you receive.

  • Flight and arrival arranged

    Your ticket is booked and arrival is organised. You will know where you are going before you travel.

  • We stay reachable after you start

    Our office does not disappear once you are placed. Message us any time you need help or advice.

  • Terms written down before you sign

    You see the salary, the role and the employer in writing. Ask us about any line you do not understand.
