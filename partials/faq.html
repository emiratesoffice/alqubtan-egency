Questions we are asked often

Do I have to pay anything to apply?

No. Applying is free, and we do not charge workers a placement fee at any stage. If anyone asks you for money in our name, tell us.

Which countries do you recruit from?

The Philippines, Indonesia, Nepal and Myanmar. Each of these countries sets its own rules for citizens taking domestic work abroad, and those rules change. Message us and we will tell you the current position for your country instead of guessing.

What documents will I need?

A valid passport, passport photographs, and the exit clearance documents your government requires. If you have certificates or references from earlier work, send those too.

How long does the process take?

It depends on your country's clearance process and on visa timing, so it is usually a matter of weeks rather than days. We will give you a realistic estimate once we know your situation.

Where will I be working?

With a family in the United Arab Emirates. Our offices are in Al Ain and Ajman, and we tell you the emirate and the household details before you agree to anything.
