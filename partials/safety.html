Protect yourself

These rules apply to us and to every other office. Please hold us to them.

  • You never pay a recruitment fee. The employer carries the cost of your visa, flight and medical check.
  • Never hand your original passport to anyone before you have signed a contract. It stays yours after you arrive.
  • Read the contract before you sign it. Ask us to explain any line you are unsure about.
  • Your salary must be paid through a bank account in your name.
  • Complete the legal exit process in your own country. Anyone telling you to travel on a visit visa and sort it out later is putting you at risk.
